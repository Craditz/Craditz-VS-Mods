## How to agree

Before merge, use the acceptance statement in the pull-request template. A
manual comment with the same statement also works as the project's acceptance
record. Each contributor must assent for the work they have authority to
license; one submitter cannot bind unrelated contributors merely by opening a
pull request.

Record the Contributor Terms version and immutable terms commit or archived
copy, contributor account, date, contribution commits and acceptance permalink.
Link to that fixed terms revision in the acceptance request. Confirm acceptance still covers the final
changes being merged. If the rights or terms change, get a fresh acceptance.
Do not merge a blank checkbox or treat silence as agreement.
