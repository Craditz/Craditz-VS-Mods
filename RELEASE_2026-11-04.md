## what changed in the den

- **Core 1.2.39:** the grazers can put dry grass into firepits and pit kilns again. construction stays construction when use ends. rig-based Bixo/Carried checks, aquatic breathing and the accepted Core 1.2.37 features come along.
- **Abilities 0.3.4:** predator trails get their own orange tint. Y is the default scent shortcut; existing custom bindings stay yours.
- **Dinosaurs 0.4.3, Enemies 0.2.2, Hooved 0.5.4 and Small & Odd 0.5.5:** the accepted camera, carry, original-texture/bleat and salmon/group-remap work has arrived in the public source.
- **Kinship 0.5.7:** Felinae 0.2.16 has separate adult and baby files; the kin-aware tasks now go to the right places. admin overrides from 0.5.6 stay. the executable is unchanged from 0.5.6.
- **Companions 0.5.100:** growth can hand the animal over to its next body while retaining its identity and intermediate-stage points. thanks to Shimmercat for spotting the missing guard. animals already Held by an earlier failed growth transition still need separate recovery. the accepted 0.5.99 work and public save/return safeguards are retained.
- **Radio 1.2.3:** playback seeks and snapshot corrections leave more useful tracks in the log. this is diagnostic work, not a claim that every jumping-song report is resolved.
- **Toolsmith 1.1.0:** 60 animal fittings work with either Toolsmith 1.2.19+ or LTO BlackSmith Plus 3.0.1+, including LTO hand/workbench assembly and supported flint axe/hoe heads. install exactly one provider with its dependencies. switching providers does not convert existing saved modular items.
- **Emotes 0.2.15:** the model-aware Shift+K pose menu and expanded Fox review controls now have a public source home.
- **Druidry 0.1.1:** the Circle of Forms, thirteen spirit families and Ritual Altar workflow now have a public source home too. Companions is not a dependency.
