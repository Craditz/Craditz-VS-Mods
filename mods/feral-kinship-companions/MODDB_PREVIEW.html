Feral Kinship: Companions

Release 0.5.94 adds Optimum-aware save compatibility for the tested Vintage Story 1.22.7 / Optimum 0.3.21 method shapes, with vanilla support retained. Native safety checks stay enabled. Existing public 0.5.93 gameplay and schema 61 are unchanged.

Gives your tamed animals personalities, conversations, jobs, talents, and expeditions. They can help around camp, travel with you, and occasionally stand nearby judging your decisions.

Living with a pack

Companions have 16 personalities, changing moods, relationships, and over 8,000 authored dialogue lines. Empty-hand right-click an owned companion to open its journal; use the whistle for nearby pack commands.

While following a stationary player, they can wander a little, look around, walk over and sit watching you, or get close and lie down for a while. Occasionally they pace between two points. Moving on or giving them something more important to do interrupts the idle behavior.

Requests, levels, and talents

Check in for requests based on the animal and its surroundings. Completed requests give Companion Points and shared Pack Points. Duties, expeditions, growing up, and helping defeat hostiles also earn experience. Individual talents cover combat, survival and mobility, and social and economy; the pack board supplies shared upgrades.

The Bonuses tab shows each native animal's free expedition specialty. Animal-specific Social talents can improve it, and a species that makes up more than half an expedition party can provide a group synergy. Solo animals and tied groups do not get a synergy.

Wellbeing and care

Open the Care Card directly with the Wellbeing button on your companion's Social tab. The wellbeing Care Card shows food, home and rest, health, safety and shelter, company and enrichment, workload, expedition outcomes, and progress. Favourite foods and comfort beds support recovery. Wellbeing affects readiness for ordinary work; food gathering and essential care remain available during recovery.

Food, work, and storage

Companions eat suitable food from Dining Boards or by hand. Hunger is non-lethal, but an empty food meter stops ordinary activities until they have eaten or recovered. Dining Board consumption pauses while the owner is offline. The food system can be disabled.

Craft raw meat or vegetable kibble with flour and two matching foods, then dry it in a cooking pot for much longer storage. Meat and vegetable kibble follow their corresponding companion diets.

Adults assigned duties work while At Ease. They can gather dropped items, rocks, plants, ripe crops, berries, fruit, mushrooms, and other enabled resources. Logging Carts coordinate tree cutting. Commands, danger, injuries, rest, and expeditions take priority.

Use the Pack Ledger to assign storage categories, or Advanced Mode for exact-item rules. Companions deliver to compatible containers and can sort misplaced cargo. Adult companions visibly carry duty cargo in their mouths.

Companions do not automatically pick up animal corpses, including through the Pack Cart’s take-with-companions control. Butcherable bodies and storage rules were not a useful combination.

Optional crop reseeding, fertilizer tending, and finite trough refilling use actual seeds and supplies. Enable these duties per companion; claims, home and wellbeing readiness still apply. In-progress trough cargo survives saving.

Backpacks and deliveries

Adult companions can equip one vanilla or compatible modded bag. Crouch-right-click the companion to open its backpack: the bag slot and its contents are shown together. Capacity and item restrictions come from the bag you equip.

You manage the backpack contents yourself. Use Go home and unload to send the companion back to unload, or Go home, unload and come back to have it come back afterwards. Deliveries go to General storage at the Pack Cart. Anything that does not fit stays in the bag. Homeward Bound reduces return time by 15% per rank, up to three ranks.

The pack roster shows the equipped bag, used slots, and delivery status, so you can keep track of companions sent home with supplies.

Commands, combat, and rescue

Choose activity, follow distance, rest, home behavior, risk tolerance, and Passive, Defensive, Protective, Assist, Aggressive, or Flee combat styles. Companions do not attack players or other owned companions.

A lethal hit normally leaves a companion mortally wounded. Hold Ctrl and use a compatible healing item during the rescue window. Talents can improve recovery and survival.

Expeditions

The Pack Cart manages expedition parties, routes, preparations, reports, cargo, and missing companions. Routes include foraging, hunting, scavenging, patrols, recruitment, and more dangerous trips. Parties can return with supplies, injuries, recruits, or missing members. Search expeditions can look for those left behind. Two parties can operate at once by default; Many Trails adds more slots.

Scouting discovers fictional sites that stay on your pack's map. Send scouts to learn the clues, then search a site for supplies with a chosen salvage focus. Keep up to three sites at a time and archive one when it runs dry. Sites have different possible finds and dangers; the Unknowable site can be searched more times than an ordinary one. Hunting trips can also bring back butcherable bodies when Butchering is installed. Without it, new hunts return raw-meat rewards; removing Butchering no longer causes new hunts to return missing-item carcasses. Previously stored missing items are not converted.

Supported companions retain their original native body while Away. Away bodies reject ordinary damage and suppress animal sounds and companion dialogue. Return handles unloaded chunks, restart and interrupted placement without replacement bodies or duplicated cargo and rewards. Recruitment also supports source-derived FOTSA and Critters families.

Breeding and young companions

Breeding is opt-in for supported foxes, hares, hyenas, wolves, goats, pigs, sheep, and compatible dogs, cats, and Fox Taming foxes. Adults need breeding enabled, At Ease, and beds in a suitable shared room. Pregnancy attempts require the owner online; a due birth waits for the owner to return.

Supported FOTSA breeding uses source family and sex definitions, gestation, litter sizes and cooldowns. Native and companion pregnancies are coordinated, with birth completion saved to avoid duplicates. Adult FOTSA companions receive the existing 10 HP minimum without reducing higher native bases or healing existing injuries. Explicitly mapped adult FOTSA species participate in the existing expedition specialties and training bonuses.

Young animals retain their source appearance and juvenile behavior, with safe requests, basic commands, and persistent identities. Talent progress is banked until adulthood.

Bramble and in-game help

Bramble is an optional animal guide who helps with taming and looking after the pack. Choose a supported animal form and set hints to Quiet, Occasional, or Frequent. The question-mark buttons and Handbook also explain the companion screens.

Companion flaps

New passive flaps occupy exactly 2x2 or 3x3 blocks overall, with the extra side and top frame removed. One item places the whole flap; breaking a participating part removes that flap and returns one item. The 1x1 flap stays unchanged, and existing oversized structures retain their compatibility path. Actual animal passage and room sealing for the new geometry still need in-game acceptance.

Interface and recovery

Expedition return locations

  1. Safe ground beside the owner's validated Pack Cart.
  2. The companion's still-assigned, verified bed if cart placement fails.
  3. Near the player only inside that cart's current work radius in the same dimension, checked again on arrival.
  4. Remain suspended, report the blocker and retry about once per minute when placement still fails.

A confirmed missing Pack Cart prompts the owner to place returned companions near them or wait until the cart is replaced. Closing the popup chooses waiting. Unloaded or unverifiable carts are not declared missing. Normal returns preserve original animals and native cargo; they do not reconstruct animals or replay rewards. Permanent chunk loading is not enabled.

Server admins can use /companionlink to preview and confirm a native-ID relink when a saved record and one verified existing body share the same owner, companion ID and exact entity code. It keeps that body and its current inventory, health and progression. Conflicting identities, unknown or unloaded source evidence, pending recovery and incompatible checkpoints remain protected. Per-animal snapshots and a saved journal precede the reference change. Reference-only Undo preserves newer body state and intentionally restores the previous reference disagreement.

Automatic native-ID relinking is off by default. When explicitly enabled, it uses the same checks and journal in bounded batches, including offline owners. It does not force-load the world or perform Emergency reconstruction. Completed or undone relink histories are excluded from new automatic repairs in this implementation. Recovery details use measured pages, and Status details retains full text behind compact roster summaries.

This version writes ledger schema 61 and accepts known schemas 57-60, including both prior private recovery and return previews. Older writers reject 61. Keep a complete pre-update world backup with matching mods; rollback requires that backup, not a DLL swap.

Social shows Species directly beneath Gender, using the localized native or source-animal name where available. Unloaded records use the saved entity code and explicitly identify saved or unavailable information.

Recovery diagnostics report the complete saved entity code, exact registration, lookup sources, saved versus actual identity fields and specific blockers. With no body, identity checks say unavailable. Bodies found in loaded chunks or retained caches still receive conflict and duplicate checks. Emergency force reconstruction accepts confirmation despite identity, history, missing-original, checkpoint and scan uncertainty. The confirmed creation uses the exact saved species code; an unavailable definition produces a concrete creation failure without substituting another species. Use /companiondiag id:<companion-id> for a read-only report.

Journals cover the pack, duties, storage, backpacks, talents, breeding, expeditions, and help. The Pack Cart has a clearer roster, Scavenging flow, party planner, cache, routes, and story reports with an exact ledger. Its cache pictures show the actual game items. Four themes and adjustable text settings are saved per player.

Vanilla companions have simple blocky body pictures and matching face portraits, with coat or sex differences where they matter. Supported compatibility animals use one shared portrait. Mission and site pictures use the same playful style, except for the deliberately ominous Unknowable door.

Restore animal finds the original at its saved location and brings that same animal to safe loaded space near its owner in the same dimension. A living original is not healed or replaced. Completion waits for native registration and command availability; pending arrival, unavailable space, timeouts, unloaded cart and different-dimension failures have distinct feedback. Genuine identity conflicts and foreign ownership remain protected. 103 controlled groups passed; affected-server, FotSA/PetAI and multiplayer gameplay remain unverified.

The developer panel and Companion Recovery Ledger expose Rebuild, Rebuild all, Emergency rebuild and Emergency rebuild all. All applies to the selected owner's confirmed roster. Rebuild preserves usable identity, appearance, progression, talents, relationships, equipment and native cargo, restores hunger and health, and clears temporary animation, movement, away/MIA, expedition and pending recovery state. It can rebuild a healthy animal and remove one from an expedition without a later expedition teleport.

Emergency rebuild uses readable native data and saved fields, then defaults missing or invalid values. It requires server admin authority. Missing animal definitions or real creation/storage failures are reported without substituting another species. Stale recovery history does not block either rebuild path. Backups and duplicate protection run automatically, uncertain cargo remains separate, and bulk recovery continues after individual failures. Confirmations are single use and expire after 60 seconds. Internal administrative Undo and diagnostics remain available.

Testing limitations: the owner tested both rebuild paths on healthy animals and observed expedition detachment without a later teleport. Controlled tests cover handlers, migration, delayed callbacks and save/reload. Broader multiplayer expedition returns, dedicated-server save/restart, optional-provider behavior, rendered UI and abrupt native-database crash ordering remain unverified. Eleven older Undo fixture checks fail identically on public 0.5.92 and this package; they are not claimed fixed. Practical server testing remains for after release.

Settings

In ModConfig/FeralKinshipCompanions.json:

  • EnableCompanionFoodSystem = true: enables companion hunger.
  • EnableAutomaticNativeIdRelink = false: opt in to guarded existing-body identity relinking on the server. Restart after changing this setting; pending approved repairs can still resume when it is off.
  • AllowCompanionDoorUse = false: enable to let companions open permitted doors and close them after passing.
  • EnableBramble = true: enables the guide.
  • StfuMode = false: enable to turn off companion dialogue.
  • IdleSounds = true: enables restored adult source-animal idle sound tasks. It does not disable native random idle sounds.
  • AmbientIdleSoundChanceMultiplier = 0.25: reduces the chance of a native random idle cue after the source animal's own roll.
  • AmbientIdleSoundMinCooldownSeconds = 60 and AmbientIdleSoundMaxCooldownSeconds = 120: randomized real-second cooldown and initial stagger per animal. Native client and restored server-task budgets are separate; restored tasks emit once per run. Warning, panic, hurt, death, combat and command sound paths remain available.

Restart the server after settings changes. Extra duties are opt-in per companion. Favourite-food candidates default to ["*:*"]; reseeding unlock/pause wellbeing defaults to 80/75, fertilizer nutrient target to 80 and maximum overshoot to 10.

Custom name pools are in ModConfig/FeralKinshipCompanionNames.cfg. Per-player themes and text settings are saved in ModConfig/feralkinshipcompanions-ui.json.

Requirements and compatibility

Optimum compatibility was checked with headless save/restart tests and a low-graphics graphical smoke test. This does not qualify every optional integration, multiplayer or every Optimum build; unsupported native contracts still stop startup.

Requires Vintage Story 1.22.7, Feral Kinship 0.5.5 or later and PetAI 5.1.1 or a compatible later version. Use matching Companions packages on clients and server.

Supports the base Feral Kinship animals, with optional support for FOTSA Taming, Wolf Taming, Cats, Fox Taming, Tamables: Critters, and Fox Socks where applicable. Install the relevant source mods.

  • Art of Growing [unofficial] and Breeding Addon [unofficial]: supported modded items can appear in expedition rewards. They require Core of Arts [unofficial].
  • Butchering: hunting expeditions can return butcherable corpses. Automatic corpse pickup remains disabled.

Still in active development. Mining and Quarrying Cart jobs are not implemented. Difficult terrain and pathfinding can still need intervention, and some talents and work-site features remain experimental.
