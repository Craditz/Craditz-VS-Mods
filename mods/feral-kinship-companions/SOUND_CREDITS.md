# Feral Kinship Companions sound credits

## Third-party recordings

None. Feral Kinship Companions 0.1.137 does not bundle audio from external
sound libraries.

## Original sounds bundled with this mod

The command whistles, mortal-wound warning, Last Stand impact, and recovery cue
were synthesized specifically for Feral Kinship Companions. They contain no
recorded samples or third-party source material. Their reproducible source is
`tools/Generate-CompanionSounds.ps1`; they are distributed under the same terms
as the mod.

## Vintage Story sounds

Several cues reference unmodified Vintage Story sound assets at runtime. Those
files are owned by Vintage Story / Anego Studios and are not copied into this
package. Referenced sounds include cart creaks, cloth, planks, the reception
bell, wooden switches, books, storage sounds, item collection, the recovery
latch, and the game's tutorial-success cue.

Every companion's death voice also comes from its own entity sound table in
Feral Kinship. The game selects and plays that species- or variant-appropriate
Vintage Story sound at the animal's position when actual death occurs.
