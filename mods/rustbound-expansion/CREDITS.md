Simple entity shapes, recall rune geometry and flat entity textures are original addon assets generated by scripts/generate_assets.py. The recall rune takes visual inspiration from the user-supplied Ultima Online reference; its sprite is not distributed. Mark Satchel references the installed base-game linen sack shape/textures and recolors the model with vertex tint; no copied game art is distributed. No Rustbound binary, source or art is distributed.

The root modicon.png is the user-approved fox wizard drawing, generated with the built-in image generator. Full-resolution source: art/modicon-source.png; resize generator: scripts/generate_modicon.py. This is separate from the World of Warcraft spell icons above.

The party-blower sound is an original procedural mono recording generated by scripts/next_assets.py, encoded as Vorbis with the installed ffmpeg.

Squeaky Steps uses the WAV supplied by Craditz. Unsolicited Applause uses a trimmed CC0 recording, "Applause in a large hall or church" by eXpl0it3r: https://opengameart.org/content/applause-in-a-large-hall-or-church . Suspicious Duck uses the public HQ preview of "Soft Duck Quack" by OwennewO (CC0 1.0): https://freesound.org/people/OwennewO/sounds/719105/ . Sources, licenses and hashes: audio-sources/sources.json. Recordings are converted to mono Vorbis by scripts/meme_assets.py and decoded amplitude is checked. Tiny thunder remains the original 0.5.0 procedural recording, played once per weather cast; no new synthesized audio is made. Native rain, lightning and Corpse Explosion sound/shaders are referenced from installed game/Rustbound assets, not redistributed. The complete wizard hats, raincloud and leaf sprite are original addon geometry/art.

Holy Hand Grenade uses the user-requested Worms Hallelujah clip (Team17), sourced from https://www.myinstants.com/en/instant/worms-holy-hand-grenade-54717/ . The mirror provides no redistribution license; public redistribution rights are unverified. Source and hash: audio-sources/holy-grenade.json. Pin sound references native game audio; grenade and pin geometry are original addon assets.

Breadcrumbs renders the installed Vintage Story bread item models and textures directly. Bread artwork belongs to Anego Studios; no copies of native bread assets are bundled.
