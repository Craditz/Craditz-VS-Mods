# Tamables: FOTSA

**Vintage Story: 1.22.x.**

the Stone Age animals also wanted to come home. i have filled out the PetAI forms with a muddy paw.

PetAI taming and owned pet behaviour for supported Fauna of the Stone Age modules, with dedicated tame variants and native-form growth/riding integration. 0.9.1 retains the exact approved package, including its historical preview filename. modded-food compatibility: probably works now, not tested. the invisible bearded dragon remains unresolved.

## what is here

source version **0.9.1**, with its matching selected assets. clone with Git LFS so the models, textures and sounds arrive as files rather than pointer slips.

## bring these along

- `game`: `1.22.0` or later
- `petai`: `5.1.1` or later

## the small print by the den door

release approval and test scope are recorded in the release inventory. compiling this source checks the public build layout; it does not make the rebuilt DLL the accepted installer or prove new gameplay. downloadable installers retain their original bytes. dependency libraries stay external.
