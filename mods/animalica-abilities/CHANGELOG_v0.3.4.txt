- Predator scent trails now have their own orange tint/glow, so you can tell them apart from the ordinary trails.
- Changed the default scent-view key to Y. K was being uncooperative. N still opens the menu, and any custom binding you've set is kept.
- The scent shortcut is now registered as an overlay control.
