Animalica Body Tools 1.6.20 - changes from public ModDB 1.6.19

- Fixed missing default item-texture references for the affected optional-metal equipment.

Tool stats, recipes, durability, dismantling and sound behavior are otherwise unchanged. Dismantling, durability and audio fixes already shipped in 1.6.19 and are not new here.
Validation: scoped live provider/crafting/equipment/restart acceptance on the private composition; final production rebuild, normalized IL comparison and controlled suites passed. This is not blanket multiplayer, all-material live visual or audible-sound qualification.
