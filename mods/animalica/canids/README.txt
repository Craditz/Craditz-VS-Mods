Animalica - Foxes, Wolves & Hyenas
===================================

Modular Animalica animal pack for playable foxes, wolves, and hyenas.

The playable fox includes independent color controls for its fur, undercoat,
socks, eyes, inner ears, and tail tip, with selectable marking patterns and
optional heterochromia.

Requires Animalica Core and PlayerModelLib.
This pack intentionally does not include cross-mod compatibility patches; those belong in the later Animalica Compatibility addon.
