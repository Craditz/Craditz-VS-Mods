Animalica - Compatibility Pack 1

General optional compatibility pack for Animalica playable models.
Requires Animalica Core and PlayerModelLib. Companion mods are optional and only unlock their matching models when installed.

This pack contains integrations for Cats, The Critters Pack coral fork, Draconis, Fox Socks, Hieronymus Reptiles Collection, Feverstone Wilds, Jimothy, and Wolf Taming.
Equus playable models are not included in this version.
The Feverstone Griffon model requires Feverstone Wilds 3.0.0-rc.7 or newer. Wingworks can provide optional flight for the griffon and supported birds.
The playable Coppertail Drake uses the current Draconis 1.6.0 adult model and requires Draconis 1.6.0 or newer.
Fauna of the Stone Age integrations are provided separately by Animalica - Compatibility Pack 2 (FotSA).

To display clothing and armor on these models, turn off PlayerModelLib's "Hide clothing" option in your character/model settings. This is a per-player PlayerModelLib setting; this pack does not override it.
