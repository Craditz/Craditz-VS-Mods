Animalica - Compatibility Pack 1

Makes selected animals from other mods playable through Animalica. Install the animal mods you want; their models appear when those mods are present.

How it works

Adds PlayerModelLib character entries, playable animation mappings, clothing and carry attachment support, and first-person integration. Assigned Animalica profiles use Core’s trait and diet settings. Some matching body variants share selectors to keep the character menu manageable.

Includes three playable dog models from Wolf Taming. Their animation mappings use the dog animations.

Requirements and compatibility

Requires Animalica Core and PlayerModelLib, plus the source mods for the animals you want. This pack supplies the playable-model integration; it does not bundle the parent mods or their assets.

Supported source mods

  • Cats
  • The Critters Pack (coral fork)
  • Draconis
  • Fox Socks
  • Feverstone Wilds
  • Hieronymus Reptiles Collection
  • Jimothy
  • Wolf Taming

Equus playable models are not included in this version.

Compatible bird models can use Wingworks for flight. This pack does not provide flight itself.
