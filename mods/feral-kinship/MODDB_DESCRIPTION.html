Server-authoritative taming

Feral Kinship

A server-authoritative taming and kinship layer that makes supported animals recognise their people and keeps their behaviour grounded in vanilla PetAI.

Adult taming Optional baby taming PetAI commands Animalica-aware

Feral Kinship is built to slot into a larger Vintage Story setup without asking you to rebuild the rest of your world around it.

FEATURE

Tame the supported set

Bear, chicken, deer, moose, fox, gazelle, goat, hare, hyena, pig, raccoon, sheep, and wolf entities can receive the Kinship tame treatment.

FEATURE

A real relationship bonus

Matching Animalica players receive a 3× ordinary-food progress bonus, with perfect kinship cookies as a premium shortcut.

FEATURE

Commands without the add-on

Even without Companions, tames retain Sit, Lie, Stay, Follow, Passive, Neutral, Protective, and Aggressive commands plus vanilla-grounded combat.

Optional animal compatibility

Galeret models count as cat kin to native Cats and the FotSA Felinae, Pantherinae, and Machairodontinae clades. Native Cats keep the existing 3× taming bonus for matching kin; FotSA recognition adds no taming support. Enable cat in an existing taming configuration to tame native Cats. Butchering prime meat and species-appropriate offal can be used for taming. Berry feeding avoids duplicate attempts when Feverstone Wilds is installed.

The useful details

What you get in-game

Feral Kinship keeps the basic layer deliberately focused: normal PetAI food rules, owner-aware targeting, rescue windows, species voices, collision-friendly tame bodies, and configurable adult/baby taming. Companions can build on top of it, but is not required.

Requirements

  • Vintage Story 1.22.x
  • PetAI 5.1.1 or newer required
  • PlayerModelLib optional for automatic model recognition. A supported synced feralKinshipRace override can provide kin recognition and bonuses without it.
  • Animalica packs optional

Good to know

Install Feral Kinship and PetAI on the server and every client, or together in singleplayer. Feral Kinship does not modify Animalica. Server configuration is authoritative in multiplayer.
