# Custom Music Radio 1.2.2 — local release, 2026-10-04

another page from the stolen computer. here is what changed in this version, with the test limits left attached.

Previous promoted version: 1.2.1
Tested optimization label: 1.2.1

- Prepare physical-radio audio on one bounded worker; return engine work to the main thread and reject stale completions.
- Bound preparation to eight pending track keys and 32 waiters per key. Pace transfers to eight 32-KiB chunks per 50-ms tick, cancel stale listeners and retain at most 16 concurrent requests with a conservative 256 MiB retained-byte budget.
- Evict compressed cache entries individually by last use, with at most four tracks and 128 MiB retained. Keep the decoded client cache and explicit debug probe behavior.
- Retain previously released host music-folder creation, seed-marker preservation, physical controls, loop modes and positional playback. The owner accepted audible Radio operation with one loaded track.

the loose ends that came with this release
The owner tested one track only. This does not establish multitrack, long-track or playback-backjump correctness, and no backjump fix is claimed. Cloud QA narrowly passed authoritative controls, automatic loop advancement, natural No loop completion, paused restart and A/B/C/Z cache integrity; range reentry was smoke coverage. Long-track Pause was inconclusive. Pending cancellation, loaded client voice correctness, alternate dataPath, blocked-folder fixtures, dedicated-server and remote-client isolation remain unverified. Client decoding can still hitch; decoded samples remain cached for the session. The obsolete RegisterCommand warning is retained.
