## Animalica - Compatibility Pack 2 (FotSA) 0.5.3

Animalica - Compatibility Pack 2 (FotSA) 0.5.3

Restores the nineteen elephant, rhino, penguin and pangolin selectors for the
current FotSA texture folders. Adds Original / Custom body texture choices for
the eight Pantherinae models, including the Lion's separate body and mane controls.
Existing source-mod gates, custom colours, presets, rigs and animations are retained.

Use Elephantidae 1.0.21, Rhinocerotidae 1.0.28, Spheniscidae 1.0.18,
Manidae 1.0.23 and Pantherinae 1.2.17 for the corresponding models. These remain
optional providers; the manifest's required dependencies are unchanged.
Older providers with legacy texture folders need Pack 2 0.5.2.

The combined package passed scoped gameplay checks, including the tested Lion
upgrade and restart sequence. This does not claim exhaustive checks for every model.

## Validation and scope

All five approved candidates had scoped gameplay and independent source checks. FWH and Bears covered Original/Custom switching; combined Compatibility Pack 2 included the Lion upgrade and restart sequence. Requests covered Bighorn options and the optional-provider guard. Companions covered its small fixes, native first/repeat refractory firing and full restart. Broader production coverage and physical provider-animal survival are not claimed.

Final archive checks confirm root manifests, forward-slash paths, unique member names, valid CRCs and exact retention of every tested runtime/asset byte. Required dependency minima and upstream notices are unchanged. Generated supporting assets come from the exact tested archives. Private preparation labels in release documentation were replaced; only Companions required a manifest version change. Source build checks are separate from the retained release binaries.

The public Requests and Companions projects compiled with external local game/dependency references, each with zero warnings and errors. Those separate validation builds were not used in the release ZIPs.
