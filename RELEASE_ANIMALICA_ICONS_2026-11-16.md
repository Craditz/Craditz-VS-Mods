The Animalica packs have a new shared icon in the Vintage Story Mods menu. Abilities and Requests now show it too. Toolsmith gets its own fox with a wrist tool.

Each installer preserves the released gameplay files and dependency requirements for its baseline. Bears and Foxes, Wolves & Hyenas retain the original texture choices. Requests retains the bighorn options and the lynx clothing fix.

The ZIPs target Vintage Story 1.22.x. Each passed archive validation and matched the copy installed in Testing. Use the accompanying SHA256SUMS.txt to verify downloads.
